How a visit works

  1. Reach out

    Fill out the short questionnaire or call. We'll pick a time that suits you.

  2. I come to you

    I'll arrive on time and bring what I need. No hauling your computer anywhere.

  3. We fix it together

    I explain each step in plain English and take as long as you need.

  4. You keep a written note

    Before I leave, you get a simple summary of what I did and how to do it again.

Live too far away to fix Mom's iPad?

I'll go to her, sort out whatever's wrong, and make sure she's protected from scam calls and emails. Afterward, I can send you a short update so you know everything is taken care of.

You can request and pay for a visit from anywhere. Your parent just has to open the door.

Fewer "the computer is broken again" phone calls for you. More patience and less frustration for them.
