About me

Hi, I'm Jacob Epps

Born and raised in Southside Virginia. Husband and father.

I've spent my career helping people when something goes wrong. I started as a 911 dispatcher for Prince Edward County and the Town of Farmville, where staying calm, listening carefully, and talking people through a stressful moment was the whole job.

From there I moved into IT and served four years as lead help desk technician at Longwood University, training a team of student technicians and helping thousands of students, faculty, and staff with their computers, phones, and accounts. Today I run the help desk for a local power provider.

I started JAE Technical Services because I kept hearing the same thing from neighbors: getting tech help means a long drive, a phone menu, or someone talking over your head. I'd rather sit down at your kitchen table, fix the problem, and make sure you know how to handle it next time.

A few things to have ready so we can spend your time fixing, not searching.

  • Your passwords and account infoEspecially your Apple ID or Google account, email, and Wi-Fi password. Keep them nearby; I won't write them down.
  • Chargers and cablesHave the devices we're working on charged or plugged in.
  • A note of what's going wrongAny error messages, when it started, and anything you've already tried.
  • Boxes or paperwork for new devicesIf we're setting up something new, keep the box and any papers it came with.
